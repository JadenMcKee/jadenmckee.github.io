## **Leadership & Activities**

- **President of MIT Sigma Chi:** I lead the chapter’s operations, oversee a large team of student leaders, and serve as the primary liaison between the chapter, MIT, and our national organization. I coordinate member development, finances, events, risk management, and long-term planning while working to build a strong and accountable chapter culture.
- **Camp Kesem at MIT Operations Coordinator:** Camp Kesem is an organization that supports children affected by a parent's cancer through a free weeklong Summer Camp run entirely by college students. As Operations Coordinator, I dedicate 6-8 hours a week to designing and running all of the programming for our events throughout the year.
- **Varsity Track and Field Athlete:** I compete in the pole vault for MIT's Track and Field team. I rank among the best in the NCAA DIII for my event.
- **Recruitment Chairman of MIT Sigma Chi:** I lead the chapter’s recruitment strategy and coordinate the planning and execution of recruitment events. I manage outreach to prospective members, organize logistics and schedules, and work with brothers to create an engaging and welcoming experience while identifying students who align with the chapter’s values and culture.
- **Makerspace Mentor:** I volunteer 4 hours a week to MIT's Makerspace ecosystem, where I run trainings on how to use our 3D Printers and Laser Cutters. I also run open shop hours where I help students with their personal projects and any tools they may need training on.
